Last updated: June 18, 2026

If you would like to delete your TadobaSolar account and associated data, you can request this by contacting our support team directly. We currently process account deletion requests manually to verify your identity and protect against unauthorized deletion of your solar plant account.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to developertadoba@gmail.com from the email address or registered mobile number associated with your TadobaSolar account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include your registered username and the mobile number/email used during sign-up, so we can verify and locate your account.
  4. Our team will verify your identity and confirm the request before proceeding.
  5. Your account and associated data will be deleted within 7 business days of a verified request, and you'll receive a confirmation email once it's complete.

What Gets Deleted vs. Retained

Data Type Action
Account profile (username, password credentials) Deleted
Device notification tokens (FCM) Deleted
Support ticket history Deleted
Payment and billing transaction records Retained as required for legal, accounting, and tax compliance purposes, even after account deletion

Retained payment records are kept solely to meet legal and regulatory obligations and are not used for any other purpose after your account is deleted.
