Privacy Policy

TadobaSolar — by Tadoba Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tadoba Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the TadobaSolar mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: username, password (stored in encrypted/hashed form), and any contact details you provide during registration or support requests.
  • Payment-related information: records of payments, billing history, and transaction status associated with your solar plant account. We do not store raw card or banking credentials — these are processed by our backend systems solely to maintain your plant's payment and lifecycle status.
  • Device and notification data: a device push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver alerts and updates relevant to your account.
  • Support communications: any messages, attachments, or details you submit through the in-app support ticket feature.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: basic technical information (such as app version and device type) that helps us maintain stability and fix issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your access to the App.
  • To track and display your solar plant's lifecycle, billing, and payment status.
  • To send you push notifications about account activity, payment reminders, or service updates.
  • To respond to support requests and resolve issues you report.
  • To maintain the security, stability, and performance of the App.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Firebase (a Google service) for authentication infrastructure and push notification delivery. Firebase processes data on our behalf under its own security and privacy practices.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Business operations: Authorized personnel at Tadoba Solutions Pvt. Ltd. may access account and payment data strictly to provide and maintain the service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers using industry-standard practices, including encrypted password storage and access-controlled databases. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no system can guarantee absolute security, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and transaction data for as long as your solar plant account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward as required for record-keeping, legal, or operational purposes.

6. Your Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.
  • You may disable notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

TadobaSolar is intended for use by adult account holders managing solar plant services and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
