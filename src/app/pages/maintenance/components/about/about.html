What is O&M?

Operation & Maintenance (O&M) ensures your solar power system operates at peak efficiency throughout its lifecycle. We monitor, maintain and optimize your system so you get maximum energy, every day.

Continuous Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of your solar system to track performance and detect issues early.

Preventive Maintenance

Regular inspection and preventive maintenance to prevent faults and ensure system reliability.

Panel Cleaning

Professional cleaning to remove dust and dirt for maximum energy generation.

Technical Support

Expert support and fast issue resolution to keep your system running smoothly.
